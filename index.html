Who We Are

Projek Kongsi started as a small group of students who simply wanted to give back. We are a youth led initiative built on the idea of sharing what we have, whether it is time, effort, or resources with communities that need it most. What began as a simple intention grew into something meaningful because of the people behind it>

🌱

What We Do

We plan and carry out community-based initiatives such as food distribution, collaborations with established NGOs, and seasonal projects like Ramadan iftar programmes. We believe in working with partners who already understand the community, so our efforts are practical and impactful rather than performative.

Little projects, lasting impact.

Every project is an opportunity to spread kindness, support our community, and make a difference.
